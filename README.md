# Melon Snap — WebGL demo

Play in the browser: https://sburdenko.github.io/melon-snap-demo/

A crash-style arcade game made in Unity 6. Three modes: snap rubber bands onto a watermelon (TAP), shake a soda bottle (HOLD), or lift the lids and feed Greedy food, not trash (FEED). Cash out before it goes wrong.

Demo only: play money, no real bets.
